Username Investigation Basics

Goal

Understanding how usernames can reveal parts of a person’s digital footprint across different platforms.

Common Techniques

* Searching exact usernames in search engines
* Checking reused usernames across platforms
* Looking for linked emails or profile pictures
* Analysing posting behaviour and timestamps

Useful Tools

* Sherlock
* WhatsMyName
* Namechk

Ethical Note

OSINT investigations should always remain legal, ethical, and privacy-conscious.
